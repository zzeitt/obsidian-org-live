#+TITLE: Math formats / 数学格式

* Bare align environment
\begin{align}
23 &= 2.3\times 10^{1} \leftarrow \text{normalized} \\
   &= 0.23\times 10^{2} \\
   &= 0.023\times 10^{3} \\
   &= \dots
\end{align}

* Inline LaTeX delimiters
当 \(\beta=2\) 时，公式 \((23)_{10}=(10111)_{2}\) 应该渲染。

* Multi-line paren display
\(
(23)_{10}=(10111)_{2}=(1.0111)_{2}\times 2^{4}
\) 。

* Bracket display
\[
(23)_{10}=(10111)_{2}
\]

* Dollar multiline
$$
a^2 + b^2 = c^2
$$

* Dollar same line
Inline $E = mc^2$ and $$E = mc^2$$ both render.

* Equation environment
\begin{equation}
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
\end{equation}
